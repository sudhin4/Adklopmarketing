FAQs

How does the 10-day free trial work?

We understand your business, build a strategy, set up and launch your campaigns, then optimize during the 10-day period. You experience how we work before deciding whether to continue.

What if I don’t get results during the 10-day trial?

Ten days is not always enough to guarantee leads or sales. The trial lets you evaluate our research, execution, testing, optimization and communication. If you’re not satisfied, there’s no pressure to continue.

How many leads or sales do you guarantee per month?

We don’t promise a fixed number before understanding your business. Results depend on your industry, budget, offer, pricing, competition, website and sales process. We set realistic targets and optimize toward them.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract after the trial?

No. If you see value in our work, we can continue together. If not, you’re free to stop.
